Based on [the excellent nn.h video series by Tsoding](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLpM-Dvs8t0VZPZKggcql-MmjaBdZKeDMw), I coded a minimal neural network that runs on the CPU in Rust, and expanded it to train on the [MNIST dataset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MNIST_database) and recognize handwriting.
